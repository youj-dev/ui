---
title: Primitives
description: "Animate your content with reveals, text effects, counters, loops and scroll effects."
---

Use these pieces to animate content you provide: reveal it, animate its text or numbers, loop it, or link it to scrolling. Each is available in `@youj/motion` and the registry, and follows the shared Speed setting.

## Appear

<Cards>
  <Card title="Reveal" href="/docs/motion/reveal">Fade, rise, blur, scale or wipe in, on mount or in view. Exits too.</Card>
  <Card title="Stagger" href="/docs/motion/stagger">Plays a group of children in sequence on a stagger token.</Card>
</Cards>

## Text and numbers

<Cards>
  <Card title="TextSplit" href="/docs/motion/text-split">Reveals text by character, word or measured line.</Card>
  <Card title="TextEffect" href="/docs/motion/text-effect">One text renderer: split-flap, terminal, decode, blur-in or rolling reels.</Card>
  <Card title="ScrambleText" href="/docs/motion/scramble-text">Decodes toward its text through random glyphs.</Card>
  <Card title="Counter" href="/docs/motion/counter">An animated number that counts or rolls, and retargets mid-flight.</Card>
</Cards>

## Loops and scroll

<Cards>
  <Card title="Marquee" href="/docs/motion/marquee">A seamless loop that eases to a stop on hover.</Card>
  <Card title="Parallax" href="/docs/motion/parallax">Maps scroll onto translate, scale, rotate and opacity.</Card>
  <Card title="ScrollProgress" href="/docs/motion/scroll-progress">A spring-smoothed bar or ring bound to scroll.</Card>
</Cards>

For pointer effects such as [Tilt](/docs/components/tilt) and [Magnetic](/docs/components/magnetic), see Visual effects in the Components sidebar.
