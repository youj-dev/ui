## Installation & usage

<InstallTabs name="activity-rings" noImport />

Use activity rings when a few goals are tracked together and each has its own target: a day's fitness goals, a team's quotas, a quarter's key results, a learning streak. Each ring is one goal, a round-capped arc over a faint track of its color, running clockwise from twelve o'clock. Two to four rings fit. To show how one whole splits into parts, use a [Share Ring](/docs/components/share-ring). For a single score on a scale, use a [Gauge](/docs/components/gauge).

```tsx
import { ActivityRings, type ActivityRing } from '@youj/react'

const rings: ActivityRing[] = [
  { label: 'Move', value: 420, max: 500, unit: 'calories', tone: 'danger' },
  { label: 'Exercise', value: 26, max: 30, unit: 'minutes', tone: 'success' },
  { label: 'Stand', value: 9, max: 12, unit: 'hours', tone: 'info' },
]

<ActivityRings rings={rings} legend="side" />
```

A ring is `value` of `max`, with a `label`, an optional `unit` after its goal, a `tone` (accent, a chart color or a status; the chart colors in turn by default) and an `icon` drawn at its start. Rings are listed outermost first. Past its goal a ring keeps going for a second lap (it draws up to two), and the legend and the spoken value give the full share, such as 138%.

The bowl shows the first ring's share in big figures with its name, or the share of the ring being pointed at. Pass `children` to put your own content there, such as a streak or a total. At `size="sm"` (96px) the bowl is left empty. The other sizes are `default`, `lg` and `xl`.

`legend="side"` puts the legend beside the rings when there's room and under them when there isn't. `legend="below"` always puts it under them. Each row gives the ring's value, goal and share. Pointing at a row, or tabbing to it, singles out its ring, and pointing at a ring singles out its row. `format` formats the values (whole numbers by default, `{ notation: 'compact' }` for large figures).

The rings fill as they come into view. `inView={false}` fills them on mount, and `{ once, amount, margin }` sets what counts as in view. A ring that closes plays the success cue when the provider has sound on, and `sound={false}` turns that off.

<ReferenceRow title="Accessibility" summary="Labels and assistive technology">

### Accessibility

- The rings are a group, named by `label` or "Activity rings", and each ring is a `meter` named by its label, with its value text spoken in full: "Move: 420 of 500 calories, 84%". Past its goal, `aria-valuenow` holds at the goal and the text gives the real value and share.
- The legend is a list. Each row takes focus, so the keyboard can single out a ring the same way the pointer does.
- Its words come from the labels (`activityRings`, `activityRingsValue`), translated by the language packs.
- A dial turns clockwise in every script, so the rings keep their direction under RTL. The bowl and the legend follow the page.

</ReferenceRow>
