## Installation & usage

<InstallTabs name="distribution-strip" noImport />

Use a distribution strip where the question is "how slow is it, really": a service's or a route's latency, a job's run time. Every sampled request is a dot on a log-scaled strip, stacked into rows where they crowd, so the swarm is fattest where most requests land and the tail stays visible dot by dot. The percentiles (p50, p90 and p99 by default) are flagged hairlines, and the objective is a dashed line: past it the strip is hatched and its dots turn to the warning tone. For one latency against a limit, use a [Segmented Meter](/docs/components/segmented-meter); for a trend over time, a [Chart](/docs/components/chart).

```tsx
import { DistributionStrip } from '@youj/react'

// Latencies in milliseconds, one per sampled request.
<DistributionStrip samples={latencies} slo={300} label="checkout-api · last 24h" />
```

The axis runs over the whole decades around the samples (10 ms to 1 s for samples between 22 and 990 ms), with a tick for every step of each decade; pass `domain` for other ends. Latencies read in milliseconds, and in seconds from 1 s, in the provider's locale; pass `format` for your own. The header counts the share of samples within `slo`.

`percentileOf(samples, p)` gives the same interpolated percentile the strip flags, for a headline figure beside it.

<ReferenceRow title="Accessibility" summary="ARIA">

### Accessibility

- A `figure` named by its visible label (by default the labels' "Latency distribution") and described by a text summary: how many requests were sampled, each percentile's latency, and the share within the objective ("97.4% within the 300 ms SLO.").
- The dots, the ruler and the hatch are decorative (`aria-hidden`); the flags and the header are text.
- With no samples it says "No data", and there's no summary.
- The axis runs from the inline start, so right to left in RTL; the flags' figures keep their order.
- Its built-in text comes from the labels, so `<YoujProvider labels>` translates it.

</ReferenceRow>
