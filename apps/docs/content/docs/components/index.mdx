Find components by what they do. A simple component is one control or surface, like a button or an input. A composite is several of them working together, or a model of rows, series or steps with props that stay generic: a data table, a chart, a date range picker, a sidebar. Most import from `@youj/react`; effects, backgrounds, players and some other components import from `@youj/motion`. Each page shows the installation and import you need.

Classic is the default component style, with bordered panels, rounded-rectangle buttons and underline tabs; fields follow the theme, soft (a filled band, the default) or outlined. [Looks](/docs/looks) explains it and Bezel, the other, with sectioned cards, filled field wells and pill actions, and [its anatomy](/docs/looks#the-anatomy) the shared design rules.

For whole pieces of a product built from components, see [Blocks](/docs/blocks/activity-and-conversations). For motion you put on your own elements, such as entrances, text effects and the transitions inside components, see [Motion](/docs/motion). For icons, see [Icons](/docs/icons), and for isometric scenes, [Illustrations](/docs/illustrations).
