## Installation & usage

<InstallTabs name="annotated-image" noImport />

Use an annotated image to say what matters in a picture: a vehicle's sensors, the parts of a product, the regions of a screenshot. Each point carries its number and a leader line runs to its label in the gutter beside the media, so the eye goes from the point along the line to the words. For a sequence of screens to step through, use a [Carousel](/docs/components/carousel).

```tsx
import { AnnotatedImage, type ImageAnnotation } from '@youj/react'

const notes: ImageAnnotation[] = [
  { id: 'reefer', x: 71, y: 34, title: 'Reefer unit', detail: '34.1°F · set 34°F' },
  { id: 'load', x: 40, y: 44, title: 'Load', detail: '14 of 26 pallets' },
]

<AnnotatedImage src="/truck.jpg" alt="Refrigerated truck, side view" annotations={notes} />
```

A point sits at `x` and `y`, from 0 to 100 across and down the media. Its label goes in the gutter on the point's side, or the one `side` names; labels in a gutter keep their order and are nudged apart so they never overlap, and they follow the media as it resizes. Under 40rem of its own width the gutters fold into a numbered list under the media, without lines. Pass `media` instead of `src` for a video, an illustration or a canvas, and `caption` for a line under the figure.

Hovering or focusing a callout, or hovering its label, lights the pair. Pressing a callout pins it: `value`, `defaultValue` and `onValueChange` hold the pinned id, or `null`.

<ReferenceRow title="Accessibility" summary="Figure, callouts, list">

### Accessibility

- It is a `figure`; `caption` is its `figcaption`. Give the image its `alt`, or your `media` its own name.
- Callouts are buttons in number order, each named by its number and described by its label; pressing one sets `aria-pressed`. They have a wider hit area than the disc.
- The labels are an ordered list named "Annotations" (`annotations` in the labels); the lines are decorative.
- The media never mirrors, so neither do the points; in RTL the labels' text runs right to left in the same gutters.

</ReferenceRow>
