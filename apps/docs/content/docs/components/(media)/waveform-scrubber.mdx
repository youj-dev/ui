## Installation & usage

<InstallTabs name="waveform-scrubber" noImport />

Use a waveform scrubber as the seek bar of a podcast, a voice note, a lecture or a workout recording, anywhere the shape of the sound helps people find their place. The loudness samples you pass become bars: played in ink, the rest dim. Chapter starts are ticks with their titles above, a ruler of minutes runs underneath, and hovering shows the time and chapter under the pointer. Drag anywhere on the wave, or use the keys: a Base UI slider does the work underneath. With `clip` it becomes a range with two accent handles, zoomed to a window round the clip, for sharing a moment. It decodes no audio: bring the samples from your pipeline, or decode a file once and store them.

```tsx
import { WaveformScrubber } from '@youj/react'

<WaveformScrubber
  // Loudness from 0 to 1, spread evenly over the recording.
  peaks={peaks}
  duration={audio.duration}
  value={time}
  onValueChange={setTime}
  onValueCommitted={(t) => (audio.currentTime = t)}
  chapters={[
    { start: 0, title: 'Cold open' },
    { start: 192, title: 'The brief' },
  ]}
/>
```

`duration` is the recording's length in seconds and `value` the playhead. `onValueChange` follows the drag frame by frame; `onValueCommitted` fires when a drag or a key press ends, the moment to seek a real player. The samples are resampled to the bars that fit (one every 5px, or `bars` of them), each bar taking the loudest sample in its slice so short peaks survive; there are never more bars than samples.

`clip` (a `[start, end]` range in seconds) turns the scrubber into two handles in the accent, with `onClipChange` reporting each move. While it clips, the wave zooms to a window round the range with at least a minute either side, and the handles step by the second. `formatTimecode(seconds, locale)` formats times the way the scrubber does (0:42, 12:34, 1:02:10), for the labels around it. The [Podcast](/docs/blocks/media) block puts it together with a player, a speed control and chapter rows.

<ReferenceRow title="Accessibility" summary="Keyboard">

### Accessibility

- The playhead is a slider named by `label` (default: the labels' "Seek"), whose value is spoken as the time, the length and the chapter ("12:34 of 42:10, Night shift").
- Arrow keys step by `step` seconds (5), Page Up and Page Down ten times as far, and Home and End jump to the ends. A clip's handles, "Clip start" and "Clip end", step by the second.
- With sound on, crossing into another chapter while scrubbing plays `tick` (`sound={false}` keeps it quiet).
- Time runs left to right in every script, like a tape, so the wave keeps its direction under RTL, the arrow keys included; the text around it follows the page.
- Its words come from the labels (`waveformSeek`, `waveformPosition`, `waveformClipStart`, `waveformClipEnd`), translated by the language packs.

</ReferenceRow>
