<Story id="options" label="Options">

<ComponentPreview name="tilt/options" height={330} />

Templates with different rotation, glare and scale settings.

`max` is the rotation, in degrees, at the edges: the pointer's position across the surface maps linearly from `-max` to `max` on both axes. `perspective` is the CSS perspective distance, where lower is more dramatic. `scale` grows the surface while it's hovered, and `glare` adds a soft highlight that follows the pointer.

The glare is a pre-drawn gradient, twice the surface's size, that translates under a soft-light blend. It never repaints, so it stays on the compositor. Its color follows the theme.

</Story>

<Story id="depth" label="Depth">

<ComponentPreview name="tilt/depth" height={330} />

A card whose icon and text sit at different depths in front of the surface.

Tilt's inner layer preserves 3D. Keep `transform-style: preserve-3d` on the way down, and avoid `overflow: hidden` in between, which flattens it. Then children can sit in front of the surface with `translate-z-*`, and they shift against it as it turns.

</Story>

## Installation & usage

<InstallTabs name="tilt" noImport />

Use it on one showcase surface that invites a closer look: a ticket, a pricing card, a product shot. Keep it off grids of everyday cards, text-heavy panels, where tilted type is harder to read, and anything people click with precision. For a lighter touch on many cards, use a [Spotlight](/docs/components/spotlight).

Wrap the surface, and put the rounded corners on Tilt itself. Its inner layer inherits them, so the glare clips to the same shape.

```tsx
import { Tilt } from '@youj/motion'

export function Ticket() {
  return (
    <Tilt glare scale={1.02} className="rounded-2xl">
      <div className="rounded-[inherit] border bg-surface p-5">…</div>
    </Tilt>
  )
}
```

### Motion

<MotionSpec
  rows={[
    { moment: 'Tilt', note: '`rotateX` and `rotateY`, up to `max` degrees at the edges', spring: 'smooth' },
    { moment: 'Hover scale and glare', note: 'grow and fade in together', spring: 'smooth' },
    { moment: 'Return', note: 'springs flat when the pointer leaves', spring: 'smooth' },
  ]}
  reduced="Flat and still: no rotation, no scale and no glare."
/>

`spring` swaps the default for another spring token. Every value is a transform or an opacity, so a tilt never triggers layout or paint.

#### Reduced motion

The surface stays flat and still. There's no rotation, no hover scale and no glare, and the content reads exactly as it does at rest.

</ReferenceRow>

<ReferenceRow title="Accessibility" summary="Keyboard, ARIA, focus">

### Accessibility

- Tilt adds no roles and no focus stop. The content inside keeps its semantics, and interactive children keep their focus rings.
- It responds to a mouse only. Touch and pen input scroll and tap as usual, and keyboard focus doesn't tilt the surface.
- The glare layer is `aria-hidden` and ignores the pointer.
- Keep `max` small on anything with text. Past about 10 degrees, type foreshortens enough to slow reading.

</ReferenceRow>
