A humidity control whose ticks change color as the air goes from dry to comfortable to damp.

`variant="ticks"` draws the arc as a ring of fine radial ticks, with no band or knob. The ticks from the start up to the value light in the tone and reach outward, longest at the value; the tick at the edge lights partway and the next few warm up a little, as Progress's ticks do. `ticks` sets how many there are (55 by default). `bands` change the tone by value: list them in ascending order, and the last band whose `from` the value has reached sets the tone. Dragging, the keys, `current`, `marks` and the figure work as on the default dial.

## Installation & usage

<InstallTabs name="dial" noImport />

Use a dial for a bounded value people turn: a thermostat, a volume, a fan speed, a brightness. The arc runs three quarters of the way round, from the lower left to the lower right, ticked at every step, with the value as an ink knob on the band and large in mono in the middle. Give it a measured value and the stretch between the two lights in its tone, heating up to the target or cooling down to it. For a value along a line, use a [Slider](/docs/components/slider); to show a score that isn't set by hand, a [Gauge](/docs/components/gauge). For a window of the day that may run past midnight, such as a sleep schedule, use an [Hours Dial](/docs/components/hours-dial).

```tsx
import { Dial } from '@youj/react'

<Dial
  label="Target temperature"
  caption="Heating to"
  value={target}
  onValueChange={setTarget}
  min={12}
  max={28}
  step={0.5}
  current={19.8}
  tone="warm"
  marks={[{ value: 17, label: 'Night' }, { value: 21, label: 'Morning' }]}
  format={{ style: 'unit', unit: 'celsius', maximumFractionDigits: 1 }}
/>
```

`min`, `max` and `step` bound the value, which snaps to the step; `largeStep` (ten steps by default) is what Page Up and Down take. `current` shows a measured value: a marker inside the band, "Now 19.8°C" under the figure, and the stretch between it and the value lit. `tone` colors that stretch: `warm` and `cool` suit heating and cooling, and the accent, a chart color or neutral suit anything else. `marks` put small rings on the ruler for setpoints, such as a schedule's. `format` sets how the figures read, and `caption` puts a mono capital over the figure. It's uncontrolled with `defaultValue`.

<ReferenceRow title="Accessibility" summary="Keyboard, focus">

### Accessibility

- It's a Base UI slider under the arc: a native range input named by `label` (default: the labels' "Dial"), with the measured value spoken beside the setting ("21.5 °C, now 19.8 °C"). The marks are its description ("Night: 17 °C; Morning: 21 °C").
- Arrows step, Page Up and Down take large steps, and Home and End jump to the ends. A pointer anywhere on the band drags the knob round and focuses the dial, so the keys carry on from there.
- A dial turns clockwise in every script, so the arc and its keys keep their direction under RTL: right and up raise the value. The text in the center follows the page.
- Every step plays the tick cue, like a detent, behind `sound` (on by default).
- Its words come from the labels (`dial`, `dialNow`, `dialValueNow`, `dialMark`), translated by the language packs.

</ReferenceRow>
