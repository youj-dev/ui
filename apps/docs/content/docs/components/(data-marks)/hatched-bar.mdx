## Installation & usage

<InstallTabs name="hatched-bar" noImport />

A hatch says "not settled": a baseline to compare against, an amount that's pending, projected or reserved, a run that missed its gate. `HatchedBar` is a bar of it that grows to a value, and `Hatch` is the fill on its own, for legend swatches and for a pending segment inside another bar. Keep solid for the actual amount beside it, so the two read as a pair.

```tsx
import { Hatch, HatchedBar } from '@youj/react'

<HatchedBar value={0.64} className="h-32 w-6" label="Projected usage" />
<HatchedBar value={0.72} fill="solid" tone="accent" orientation="horizontal" />
<Hatch className="size-3" />
```

`value` is out of `max` (default 1). A bar grows up from the bottom, or from the inline start when `orientation="horizontal"`; size it with classes. `fill="solid"` draws the settled amount: in `neutral` tone that's a quiet fill, so a colored bar beside it stands out. The hatch is diagonal stripes of the tone over a faint wash of it, so it follows the theme in both modes.

<ReferenceRow title="Accessibility" summary="Labels and assistive technology">

### Accessibility

- With `label` the bar is a `meter` with its orientation, and speaks its share of `max` as a percentage (or your `valueText`). Without, it's hidden from assistive technology.
- `Hatch` is always decorative: say what it marks in a legend or in the text beside it.
- A pattern is not a color, so hatched and solid stay apart in grayscale and for color-blind readers.
