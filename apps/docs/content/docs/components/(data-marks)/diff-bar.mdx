## Installation & usage

<InstallTabs name="diff-bar" noImport />

Use a diff bar to compare two values of one measure: two models on accuracy, this month's latency against last month's, a candidate against production. Each side grows out from a shared center toward its own end, A to the start and B to the end, in cells you can count, and the better side is lit. Stack them and every row shares one center line, so a table of them reads as a verdict at a glance. For one row's share of a whole use a [Share Bar](/docs/components/share-bar); for a baseline beside the actual, a [Hatched Bar](/docs/components/hatched-bar).

```tsx
import { DiffBar, diffWinner } from '@youj/react'

<DiffBar label="Accuracy" a={91.2} b={92.4} format={{ style: 'unit', unit: 'percent' }} />
<DiffBar label="Latency p95" a={1840} b={910} better="lower" format={{ style: 'unit', unit: 'millisecond' }} names={['Large', 'Mini']} />

diffWinner(1840, 910, 'lower') // 'b'
```

`better` says which way wins: `higher` (the default) or `lower`. A difference under `tolerance` of the larger value (half a percent by default) is even, and lights neither side. Each side's full length is the larger value, or your `max` when several bars should share a scale. `format` and `locale` set how the values read, `segments` how many cells each side has (14; `0` draws solid bars), and `tone` the lit side's color (the accent, a status or a chart color).

`layout` is `diverging` (the default, out from a shared center) or `stacked` (two rows on one scale, the worse side's excess in danger).

`diffWinner(a, b, better, tolerance)` returns the same verdict (`'a'`, `'b'` or `'even'`), for a chip or a count of wins beside the bars.

### Styling

Parts: `diff-bar` (with `data-winner`, and `data-variant`: `diverging` or `stacked`), `diff-bar-name` (stacked), `diff-bar-label`, `diff-bar-row`, `diff-bar-value` and `diff-bar-track` (each with `data-side`), `diff-bar-cell` (with `data-state`), `diff-bar-bar`, `diff-bar-fill` (stacked: with `data-part`, `shared` or `excess`), `diff-bar-center` and `diff-bar-verdict`. The value columns are `--diff-bar-value` wide (4.5rem), so stacked bars share one center; widen it for longer values.

<ReferenceRow title="Accessibility" summary="ARIA">

### Accessibility

- The values are text at the bar's ends, read in order, and a visually hidden verdict follows them: "Mini is better by 51%", or "Even". The margin is relative to the worse side unless you pass `by` ("1.2 points").
- `names` name the sides in the verdict (`['A', 'B']` by default).
- The cells are decorative (`aria-hidden`).
- The start side grows toward the inline start, so the bar mirrors in RTL.
- Its words come from the labels (`diffBetter`, `diffEven`), translated by the language packs.

</ReferenceRow>
