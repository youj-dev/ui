## Installation & usage

<InstallTabs name="milestone-ruler" noImport />

Use a milestone ruler for when things land across a long span: a product's year of releases, a quarter's launch plan, a grant's reporting dates. It answers "what's next, what slipped, how far along are we" at a glance and keeps one line of detail a hover away. For the order of steps in one flow use a [Stage Rail](/docs/components/stage-rail); for events and durations within one incident, a [Severity Timeline](/docs/components/severity-timeline). The [Roadmap timeline](/docs/blocks/timelines-and-steps#roadmap-timeline) card is built on it.

```tsx
import { MilestoneRuler, type Milestone } from '@youj/react'

const milestones: Milestone[] = [
  { id: 'v1-5', date: '2026-06-04', title: 'Version 1.5', description: 'New editor', major: true },
  { id: 'sso', date: '2026-10-28', title: 'SSO and audit log', status: 'at-risk' },
  { id: 'v2', date: '2026-11-26', title: 'Version 2', description: 'Rebuilt sync engine', major: true },
]

<MilestoneRuler milestones={milestones} today={today} label="Roadmap 2026" />
```

A milestone's `date` is a day: a date-only string such as `"2026-11-26"` is that calendar day in every time zone, and a `Date` or a time in ms counts by its local date. Without `start` and `end` the ruler spans January 1 to December 31 of today's year. `status` is `done`, `upcoming` or `at-risk`; left out, it is done before `today` and upcoming from it on. Pass `today` from your server so the page hydrates with the same marks; without it the ruler waits for the browser's clock. `major` draws a taller pin.

Milestones closer than about ten pixels share one pin with their count, so the grouping follows the space the ruler has. A picked milestone (`value`, or the next one from today by default) keeps its callout shown; hovering or focusing another shows that one until the pointer or focus leaves. `formatDate` writes the days your way.

### Styling

Parts: `milestone-ruler`, `milestone-ruler-callout-zone`, `milestone-ruler-callout`, `milestone-ruler-caret`, `milestone-ruler-plot`, `milestone-ruler-ticks`, `milestone-ruler-tick`, `milestone-ruler-today`, `milestone-ruler-pins`, `milestone-ruler-pin`, `milestone-ruler-dot`, `milestone-ruler-count`, `milestone-ruler-axis`, `milestone-ruler-month` and `milestone-ruler-empty`. A pin has `data-status` (`done`, `upcoming` or `at-risk`), `data-yj-major`, `data-yj-shown` while its callout is up and `data-yj-count` when it holds several milestones; a tick has `data-yj-major` at a month's start. The pins' color is `--milestone-ruler-tone`, and the count's ink `--milestone-ruler-ink`.

<ReferenceRow title="Accessibility" summary="Keyboard, focus, screen readers">

### Accessibility

- A horizontal `listbox` (your `label`, or the labels' "Milestones") described by a summary: how many milestones, and how many are done.
- Every pin is an `option` named with its title, day and status ("SSO and audit log, Oct 28, At risk"), and described by its one-line description; a shared pin names each of its milestones. The picked one is `aria-selected`.
- One tab stop. Left and Right walk the pins (mirrored in RTL), Home and End go to the ends, and selection follows focus. A click or tap anywhere on the ruler picks the nearest pin.
- The callout, ticks, today mark and month names are decorative; the options say the same in words. Status is a word and a solid, hollow or warning-toned dot, never color alone.
- Its built-in text comes from the labels, so `<YoujProvider labels>` translates it.

</ReferenceRow>
