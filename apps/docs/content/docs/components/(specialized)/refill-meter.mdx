## Installation & usage

<InstallTabs name="refill-meter" noImport />

Use a refill meter where a client, a key or an endpoint spends from a token bucket: how much it can still send right now, and how fast that room comes back. Each cell is a token (or a share of a large bucket), lit while it's in the bucket. Requests drain cells from the edge; between the updates you give it, the bucket refills on its own at `refillRate`, and the edge cell charges from its foot as the next token accrues. For a quota that only resets with the period, use an [Limit Meter](/docs/components/limit-meter); for usage of a resource, a [Segmented Meter](/docs/components/segmented-meter).

```tsx
import { RefillMeter } from '@youj/react'

// What the gateway reported: 38 tokens left at `measuredAt`, refilling 10 a second.
<RefillMeter value={38} updatedAt={measuredAt} capacity={60} refillRate={10} burst={20} />
```

Give it what you measured: `value` tokens at `updatedAt` (a `Date.now()` time; by default, when `value` last changed). It counts the refill from there, so there's no need to poll to keep it moving, and a new measurement simply resets the count. The refill stops at `capacity`, while the meter is off screen and in a hidden tab, and catches up when it's back.

It draws a cell a token up to 60 cells; a larger bucket shares its capacity across 60 (set `segments` for another count), and the charging cell fills by its share.

<ReferenceRow title="Accessibility" summary="Labels and assistive technology">

### Accessibility

- It's a `meter` (Base UI Meter) named by its visible `label` (by default the labels' "Tokens").
- Its value is spoken as "42 of 60 tokens, refilling 10 a second", with "under the burst of 20" while under the marker and "Empty" when there's nothing left. It updates a whole token at a time, never every frame.
- The cells, the marker and the charging fill are hidden from assistive tech. The bucket fills from the inline start, so right to left in RTL; its figures keep their order.
- Its built-in text comes from the labels, so `<YoujProvider labels>` translates it.

</ReferenceRow>
