## Installation & usage

<InstallTabs name="day-rail" noImport />

Use an day rail for a trip, or anything that moves between places over a few days: a tour, a site visit, a move. Each day is a column and a tab. Flights arc over the rail from departure to arrival with their code at the top, and a change of clocks is marked beside the landing (+1 h). Stays sit under the rail as spans from check-in to check-out, in their stop's color, with their nights. Past legs are solid and quiet, the ones ahead dashed, and during a flight the plane rides its arc. Pick a day and its `ItineraryDay` panel shows under the rail. The [Trip block](/docs/blocks/calendars-and-schedules#itinerary) lists each day's bookings there. For shifts across a week, use [Time Lanes](/docs/components/time-lanes); for events that already happened, a [Timeline](/docs/components/timeline).

```tsx
import { ItineraryDay, DayRail, type ItineraryLeg, type ItineraryStay, type ItineraryStop } from '@youj/react'

const stops: ItineraryStop[] = [
  { id: 'lis', code: 'LIS', name: 'Lisbon', utcOffset: 60 },
  { id: 'bcn', code: 'BCN', name: 'Barcelona', utcOffset: 120 },
]
const legs: ItineraryLeg[] = [
  { id: 'out', from: 'lis', to: 'bcn', depart: new Date('2026-09-30T08:40Z'), arrive: new Date('2026-09-30T10:55Z'), code: 'TP 1036' },
  { id: 'back', from: 'bcn', to: 'lis', depart: new Date('2026-10-03T12:20Z'), arrive: new Date('2026-10-03T14:35Z'), code: 'TP 1039' },
]
const stays: ItineraryStay[] = [
  { id: 'bonay', at: 'bcn', start: new Date('2026-09-30T13:00Z'), end: new Date('2026-10-03T09:00Z'), name: 'Casa Bonay' },
]

<DayRail stops={stops} legs={legs} stays={stays} start={new Date('2026-09-29T23:00Z')} days={4}>
  <ItineraryDay value={0}>Fly to Barcelona at 09:40.</ItineraryDay>
  <ItineraryDay value={1}>Sagrada Família at 10:30.</ItineraryDay>
</DayRail>
```

A stop is a place with a `code` for the rail, a `name` for the words, and its `utcOffset` in minutes, which sets how its times read and marks the change of clocks on landing. A leg flies `from` one stop `to` another between `depart` and `arrive`, with an optional flight `code`. A stay sits `at` a stop from `start` to `end`, and takes its stop's `tone` (the chart colors in turn by default). A flight is hours on a rail of days, so its arc is drawn at least a twelfth of the rail wide around its middle; the exact times are in the words.

`start` is the first moment shown, the first day's midnight at the first stop, and `days` how many follow (7 by default). `now` places the line: by default it follows the clock, read every 30 seconds once the page is live; pass a time to pin it, or `null` to hide it. The day shown starts on today while the trip is under way and on the first day otherwise; set it with `defaultValue`, or control it with `value` and `onValueChange`. Each `ItineraryDay` takes the `value` of its day, from 0.

<ReferenceRow title="Accessibility" summary="Keyboard, announcements">

### Accessibility

- The days are a tab list named by `label` (default: the labels' "Itinerary"); each day is a tab named by its number and date ("Day 4, Sat, Oct 3"), and its `ItineraryDay` is the tab panel.
- Left and right move between days (mirrored in RTL), Home and End go to the first and last.
- The drawing is hidden from screen readers. In its place a list gives every leg with its code, from where to where, its local times and any change of clocks ("VY 6104, Barcelona to Rome, Sat, Oct 3, 14:20 to Sat, Oct 3, 16:10"), and every stay with its stop and its nights.
- Its words come from the labels (`itinerary`, `itineraryDay`, `itineraryLeg`, `itineraryTimeShift`, `itineraryNights`, `itineraryNow` and `itineraryInTransit`), translated by the language packs.
