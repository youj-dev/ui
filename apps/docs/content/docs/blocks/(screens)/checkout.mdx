### Accessibility

Checkout has one `h1` and puts the order summary under its own `h2`, with the order as a real list; fields are labelled. Subscription checkout wraps the page in `main` under a `header`, labels its plan choices, and announces the changing total in a live region (`aria-live`). Links and buttons are real `a` and `button` elements with the kit’s focus ring.
