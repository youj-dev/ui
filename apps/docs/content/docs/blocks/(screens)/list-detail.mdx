### Accessibility

Notifications has one `h1`, tabs for its filters, a live region for new items and a preferences table with a labelled switch per channel. List and detail moves through requests with Arrow Up and Down, Home, End and Enter, reorders with Alt plus the arrow keys, reports loading and errors in status and alert regions, and closes its phone drawer with Escape. Record drawer opens a sheet that traps focus and closes with Escape. Links and buttons are real `a` and `button` elements with the kit’s focus ring.
