### Accessibility

Logistics is one page heading over a tablist of three views (Fleet, Dispatch and Shipment); each view is a tab panel, and the fleet's keys answer only while Fleet is shown. Each of the other screens is a `main` with one page heading. Fleet overview names the map as an image that says how many ships are at sea and how many are holding, keeps its panels as labeled complementary regions, announces the voyage that opens in a polite live region, and answers the keyboard: `/` searches, `J` and `K` step through the list, `+` and `−` zoom, `0` frames the fleet and `Esc` closes the voyage. Dispatch board groups its figures, names the queue and the map’s route, draws proposed runs dashed as well as in a different color, and decides every proposal with buttons that can be undone. Shipment detail lists the vehicle’s readings and the route as named lists, marks the agent’s case as an article whose state is in its `data-status`, and reads its timeline as of the demo’s clock. Links and buttons are real `a` and `button` elements with the kit’s focus ring.
