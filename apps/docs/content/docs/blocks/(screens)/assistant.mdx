### Accessibility

AI workspace has a `main` with one `h1`, a labelled history `nav` that marks the current chat with `aria-current`, a status region for progress, and a sheet for the history on phones. The streamed answer starts on its own (set `autoplay` to `false` to start it still), and under reduced motion text lands a phrase at a time and the shimmer and carets hold still. Links and buttons are real `a` and `button` elements with the kit’s focus ring.
