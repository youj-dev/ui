### Accessibility

File manager is a `main` under a `header` with labelled toolbar controls, a selection bar and an upload tray; the preview opens in a sheet that traps focus and closes with Escape, and file actions sit in menus with arrow-key navigation. Music studio’s controls are a labelled group and its arrangement is a labelled ordered list. Collaborative canvas is a `main` named by its board, with breadcrumbs, a labelled group of who is here and a Share button that reports the copied link; the board is the [Whiteboard](/docs/components/whiteboard), its shapes a listbox walked with Tab, its cards regions of their own, and each comment pin a button that opens its thread in a popover that takes and returns focus. Links and buttons are real `a` and `button` elements with the kit’s focus ring.

Meeting booking is a `main` with time slots that use `aria-pressed`, field errors in an alert and the confirmation in a status region.
