### Accessibility

Each screen sits in a `main` or a labelled region with one page heading (`headingLevel` changes it), and its panels are labelled regions or sections (`aria-labelledby`) that set `aria-busy` while they load. Analytics and Funnel report add visually hidden text for loading, ranks, trends and empty cohort cells. Spend overview states its status in words (on track, or the day it will exceed the limit), names the forecast chart with a text summary, draws the request strip as one image that says how many requests failed, and lists the audit trail and the top spenders as ordered lists. Usage shows budget warnings as alerts and labels its spend-cap slider. Money overview names its balance and cards as regions, hides every amount behind a labeled toggle that reports its state, and reviews a payment in a drawer before it sends. Links and buttons are real `a` and `button` elements with the kit’s focus ring.
