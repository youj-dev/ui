<BlockStory id="coding-course" label="Step path" block="app-step-path" story="CodingCourse" backdrop="meadow">

A course as a trail of steps in sections, each ringed by its mastery with the current one ready to start; here a coding course, halfway through Functions with today's goal already met, so the streak reads kept and the next level is 40 XP away. Beside it sit the points to the next level, the week's streak and today's goal. The same path lays out an onboarding by weeks and tasks or a fitness program by phases and workouts.

</BlockStory>

<BlockStory id="money-overview" label="Money overview" block="app-money-overview" story="Default" backdrop="seaglass">

Show a personal bank’s home: the balance with its hide-amounts toggle, a swipeable card carousel, spending by category, budgets and a day-grouped activity list, with a send-money flow that adds the payment to today.

**Demo only.** Its content is fixed in the block: it takes no data or `copy` props, only the few settings and callbacks listed in its props. Copy the source to build on it.

</BlockStory>

<BlockStory id="collaborative-canvas" label="Collaborative canvas" block="app-collaborative-canvas" story="Default" backdrop="heather">

Review work together on one board: live chart and stat cards among sticky notes and connectors, teammates’ cursors moving with the board, and comment pins that open a thread to answer and resolve, under breadcrumbs, who is here and Share.

</BlockStory>

<BlockStory id="chapter-player" label="Chapter player" block="app-chapter-player" story="Default" backdrop="meadow">

A recording as a listening instrument, whether a podcast episode, a lecture or an audiobook: chapter and clock, a waveform scrubber with chapter ticks, a transport with back 15 and forward 30, a speed fader and a Clip tool that brackets half a minute to share.

</BlockStory>
