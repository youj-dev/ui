### Accessibility

Every section in this family is a `<section>` named by its own heading (`aria-labelledby`), so it shows up as a labelled region. The heading is an `h2` by default; set `headingLevel` to `1` or `3` where the page around it needs another. Feature spotlight sets its items’ headings one level deeper.

The pictures beside the text are decorative in Feature grid, Feature steps and Feature tabs: they are `aria-hidden` and `inert`, and a feature’s title and description carry the meaning, so say in the text whatever a `media` shows. Feature bento and Feature spotlight pass `media` through as you give it, so what you put there is read: give an image its `alt`, and keep a demo’s controls labelled. Feature steps is a named ordered list, so a screen reader announces the count; each number is a button named for its step (“Step 2 of 4: Push a branch”), with `aria-current="step"` on the step in play. Its tour has a Play and Pause button beside the call to action, holds while keyboard focus is in the steps, off screen or in a hidden tab, and never runs under reduced motion, where every step shows its finished picture.

Feature tabs is built on Tabs: the tab list is named (`copy.listLabel`), Left and Right Arrow move between tabs, Home and End jump to the ends, and the panel follows the selected tab. It tours itself until someone picks a tab, and pauses while the pointer or focus is inside it, while the page is hidden or the section is out of view; it never tours under reduced motion.

Everything animated stands still under reduced motion, and the links and buttons are real `a` and `button` elements with the kit’s focus ring.
