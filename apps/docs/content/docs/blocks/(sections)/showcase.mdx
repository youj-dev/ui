**Demo only.** Its content is fixed in the block: it takes no data or `copy` props, only the few settings and callbacks listed in its props. Copy the source to build on it.

### Accessibility

Every section here is a `<section>` named by its own heading (`aria-labelledby`). The heading is an `h2` by default and `headingLevel` changes it; the pieces with items below the title (Product tour, Solutions, What’s new) set theirs one level deeper.

Product tour and Solutions are built on Tabs: the tab list is named, Left and Right Arrow move between tabs, Home and End jump to the ends, and the panel follows the selected tab. Product tour announces a finished tour in a polite status region; Solutions announces the role and industry in view the same way. Product tour’s beacon is decoration (`aria-hidden`, and it never takes a click): the step’s “Try it” prompt says the same in words, and under reduced motion the beacon is a still dot.

Integrations has a labelled search field, a categories `nav` whose buttons use `aria-pressed`, and a result count in a status region; its detail sheet traps focus and closes with Escape. What’s new’s area filter and Product comparison’s alternative picker and “only differences” switch are named controls, and Product comparison is a real `table` with visually hidden text in its cells and a labelled coverage bar. Theme playground groups its controls under a label, announces the applied recipe in a status region, and renders its preview as a decorative frame (`inert`) around a real page.

The links and buttons are real `a` and `button` elements with the kit’s focus ring.
