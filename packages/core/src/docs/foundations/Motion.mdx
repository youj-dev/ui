# Motion

Precise and kinetic: motion that starts **immediately**, travels **fast**, and lands **exactly**, with no drift and no wobble. Every value lives in `src/lib/youj/tokens.ts` and reaches components through `useYouj()`. The toolbar's **Speed** control scales all of it, and **Motion: reduced** switches every demo to its end state.

## Easing

Five curves. `standard` is the default. `enter` and `exit` are asymmetric on purpose: arrivals are at full speed from the first frame and decelerate into place, and exits accelerate away and commit.

## Springs

Springs are defined by **perceived duration and bounce**, so they scale with the Speed control like everything else. `snappy` and `smooth` are critically damped. `settle` has ζ = 0.7: one legible overshoot of about 4.6% and a second too small to see. It is reserved for success and arrival.

### The spring in CSS

`--ease-spring` is the `smooth` spring as a CSS `linear()` curve, sampled from the same physics `useYouj` hands Motion, and `--yj-duration-spring` is the time it takes to settle (640ms in precise, Speed included). Each motion personality re-values both: calm is longer, lively keeps its small overshoot. Use the pair for **transforms** that should land like a spring without JavaScript, and keep opacity on a short tween, so the element is visible at once and settles after: `yj-pop`, `yj-tooltip` and `yj-dialog` enter this way, and a Button releases its press on it. Multiply by `--yj-motion` as with every duration: `transform calc(var(--yj-duration-spring) * var(--yj-motion, 1)) var(--ease-spring)`, or the `ease-spring` utility with `duration-(--yj-duration-spring)` where depth already scales by `--yj-motion`.

## Stagger

The step between siblings. `tight` is about a frame and a half at 60 Hz, a ripple more than a sequence. `base` reads as one gesture. `loose` is for a few large items.
