New miniature: the office printer (`@youj/miniatures/office-printer`, `MiniatureOfficePrinter`), a floor-standing multifunction printer and copier. Print a report and its pages feed out one by one and stack in the center tray, each job offset from the last; lift the lid and a light bar sweeps under the glass; copy the original lying on it; pull a paper drawer out to see its ream; clear a jam through the side door. It is also a progress indicator: an export's pages follow `progress`, and `done` turns the panel green.
