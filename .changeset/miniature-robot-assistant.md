New miniature: the robot assistant (`MiniatureRobotAssistant`, `@youj/miniatures/robot-assistant`), for AI assistants, chat, copilots and onboarding helpers. A friendly desk robot with a round-edged head, a dark glass face with glowing screen eyes, an antenna lamp on a spring, a chest screen, little arms and a round base with a light ring. It blinks and follows your pointer. Type and it listens, its eyes jumping to each key as your words appear on its chest; press Enter and it thinks, the antenna pulsing and its eyes squinting and rolling, then it types a short reply on its chest screen with a smile. Click it to wave hello, and the number keys set a mood: happy, surprised, puzzled or sleepy. It is also a progress indicator: a page can drive its thinking with `progress` while an answer streams, and `done` ends it with a smile. With reduced motion it jumps between its poses and shows each reply whole.
