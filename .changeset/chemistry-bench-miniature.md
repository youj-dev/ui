New miniature: the chemistry bench, `MiniatureChemistryBench` from `@youj/miniatures/chemistry-bench`. A distillation train on a lab bench: a round-bottom flask clamped over a gas burner, a Vigreux column with a thermometer, a coil condenser and a conical receiving flask, with test tubes in a rack and a beaker on a stirring hot plate. The liquid boils, vapor climbs the column, and drops condense and drip into the receiver, which fills as the flask runs down. Space lights the burner or puts it out, the arrow keys set the heat, a test tube (click it, or press its number) pours into the beaker with a color change reaction, and S spins the stir bar. It is also a progress indicator, for processing and AI generation: `progress` fills the distillate to it, and `done` lets the last drops run in, puts the burner out and lights the receiver. With reduced motion it holds still and jumps between states.
