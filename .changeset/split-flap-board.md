New miniature: the split-flap board, `MiniatureSplitFlapBoard` from `@youj/miniatures/split-flap-board`. A desk-sized departures board whose flaps clatter through their drums and settle, column by column, on a status word, the local time, uptime, a deploy and what's new. Space or → flips ahead, ← back, A turns auto advance on or off, and H runs every drum home. With reduced motion the characters change without the flip.
