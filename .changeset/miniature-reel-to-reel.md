New miniature: the reel-to-reel tape recorder, `MiniatureReelToReel` from `@youj/miniatures/reel-to-reel`, for audio, podcast, voice-note and recording screens. An upright studio deck with two machined reels, the tape running over its guides and heads, backlit VU meters and a record lamp. Space plays or stops it. The tape runs at a steady speed, so each reel turns as fast as its own pack needs as the tape moves from one to the other. R records, lighting the lamp while the needles swing on the input, and punches out again. The arrows rewind and fast-forward, and T threads a fresh reel of blank tape. It's also a progress indicator: the tape travels from one reel to the other with `progress`, and `done` winds the rest through. With reduced motion the reels and needles hold still and the deck jumps between states.
