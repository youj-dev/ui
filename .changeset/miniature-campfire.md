New miniature: the campfire and tent (`MiniatureCampfire`, `@youj/miniatures/campfire`), for offline, break and "come back later" screens, maintenance pages and calm or wellness apps. A canvas ridge tent with a storm lantern hanging in its door, a stone-ringed campfire, a log seat and a roasting stick on a patch of turf. The fire crackles, sends sparks up and burns down slowly to embers; Space adds a log, and it flares up, then settles. Click the tent, or press L, to light or dim the lantern. Drag the stick to the fire, or press T, to toast the marshmallow from golden to burnt, and M puts on a fresh one. In dark mode the fire lights the camp and the stars come out. With reduced motion the flames hold still, no sparks fly, and every change jumps to its new state.
