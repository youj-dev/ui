New miniature: the traffic light (`MiniatureTrafficLight` from `@youj/miniatures/traffic-light`), a status light for blocked, pending and ready states, queues and checks. A street signal on its pole with visored red, amber and green lamps, a pedestrian push-button box and a walk signal with a countdown. The lights cycle red, green, amber on a controller's timing. Space or the button box lights WAIT and cuts a green short, then the walking figure shows while the countdown runs. R, A and G hold red, amber or green, N flashes amber for the night and C resumes the cycle. With reduced motion the lamps switch without fading, and the flashing hand and night amber stay lit.
