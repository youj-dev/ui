New miniature: the model train (`@youj/miniatures/model-train`, `MiniatureModelTrain`), a model railway on a wooden baseboard. A crimson tank engine and its coaches run a timetable round a ballasted oval, calling at a country station and a halt; the barriers of a level crossing come down and its lights flash as the train comes. Space departs it or stops it at the next station, the arrows open and close the throttle with smooth acceleration and braking, S sets the home signal to danger so the train brakes and waits at it, and W blows the whistle. It is also a progress indicator: the trip from one station to the next follows `progress`, and `done` brings the train in with the platform lamps turning green.
