New miniature: the rocket launch pad (`MiniatureRocketLaunchPad` from `@youj/miniatures/rocket-launch-pad`), for deploys, launches, going live and onboarding finish lines. Space starts the count: the board flips down from ten, the tower's arms swing away and the deluge sprays, the engines ignite and the rocket climbs out of the frame on its flame while smoke billows from the trench; then a fresh rocket rolls back out to the pad. Esc aborts the count and the arms swing back. It's also a progress indicator: `progress` drives the count and `done` lifts the rocket off. With reduced motion it jumps between those states, with no flicker and no drifting smoke.
