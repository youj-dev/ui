New miniature: the lava lamp, `MiniatureLavaLamp` from `@youj/miniatures/lava-lamp`. A tapered glass globe of wax and water on a brushed aluminum base, for idle states, ambient and focus modes and empty screens. Warm wax lifts off the pool on the bottom and rises in slow blobs that stretch, merge and split, then cools under the cap and sinks back, with a soft glow in the dark. Space switches it on and off: the bulb lights at once, but the wax warms slowly, sluggish before it flows, and settles as it cools. The arrow keys change the colors of the wax and the water, and a click on the glass, or Enter, nudges the flow. With reduced motion it holds a still picture of the flow and jumps between states.
