New miniature: the popcorn machine (`MiniaturePopcornMachine`, `@youj/miniatures/popcorn-machine`), for loading states, celebrations, entertainment and media. A cinema popcorn machine on the counter: a red cabinet of glass with a steel kettle hanging from the roof, a heat lamp, a door and a scoop. It heats and pops on its own: the first kernels pop slowly, then in a crackling rush that lifts the kettle's lids and tumbles popcorn onto the heap, and the empty kettle tips forward to dump the rest. Left running, the cabinet fills until it overflows and the door bursts open onto the counter. Space switches the kettle's heat off and on, N drops a new batch, B drizzles butter, and S or a click on the door scoops a box. It's also a progress indicator: the heap fills with `progress`, and `done` pops it to the brim and over. With reduced motion it jumps between states instead.
