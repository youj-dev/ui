New miniature: the security camera, `MiniatureSecurityCamera` from `@youj/miniatures/security-camera`. A pan-tilt camera on a wall bracket that follows your pointer across the floor, throwing a soft pool of light where it looks, and patrols when the pointer leaves. A fast movement logs a motion event, the lens snaps to focus and the floodlight comes on. The arrow keys pan and tilt it, Z zooms, N switches to infrared night vision, P slides the privacy shutter over the lens, and Space takes a snapshot. With reduced motion it holds still at home and jumps to the pointer instead of panning.
