New miniature: the drawbridge (`MiniatureDrawbridge`, `@youj/miniatures/drawbridge`), for gates, approvals, permissions, access requests and reviews. A white double-leaf bascule bridge over a canal, with balance beams and their counterweights on its portals, road barriers with signals, red and green span lamps, a brick control tower, and a sailing boat too tall for the closed bridge. Press Space or click the tower to approve: the barriers come down and the road signals turn red, the leaves rise as the counterweights swing down, the span lamps turn green and the boat passes, then everything returns and the next boat comes up. Escape denies: the boat holds and sounds its horn, and an opening still under way comes back down. It's also a progress indicator: the opening follows `progress`, and `done` lets the boat through. With reduced motion it steps between its states without travel.
