New miniature: the mechanical keyboard (`MiniatureMechanicalKeyboard` from `@youj/miniatures/mechanical-keyboard`), for dev tools, keyboard shortcuts, typing and command palette screens. Type on your keyboard and the matching keycap travels down and springs back while an RGB ripple spreads from it across the keys and the underglow. Caps lock lights its LED, the arrows turn the volume knob (the F-row shows the level) and step the lighting through ripple, wave, breathe and off, and the space bar rocks on its stabilizer. A shortcut such as ⌘K shows on its keycaps and still reaches the page.
