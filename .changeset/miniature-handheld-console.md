New miniature: the handheld game console (`MiniatureHandheldConsole` from `@youj/miniatures/handheld-console`), for gaming, achievements, rewards and playful empty states. A retro handheld leaning back on a small stand, with an amber backlit screen, a d-pad, A and B, select and start, a speaker grille and a cartridge in its top edge. It plays Pip Hop, a little game of its own: walk Pip under the falling coins with the arrows, hop the blocks that roll in with A and dash with B, while the buttons press under your keys and the score shows in the readout. Start pauses, select switches the backlight, and E ejects the cartridge and pushes it back in, so the console boots again. Left alone it plays a demo; with reduced motion the demo holds still and the game takes one step per move instead of running on its own.
