New miniature: the water wheel mill, `MiniatureWaterWheel` from `@youj/miniatures/water-wheel`, for processing pipelines, batch jobs, energy and sustainability screens. A small cutaway mill: a wooden overshot wheel fed by a flume from a stone pier, its shaft running into the millhouse, where a pit wheel, a wallower and a great spur wheel turn a stone nut and the runner stone, and flour runs down the meal spout into a sack. Space, or a click on the sluice gate, opens or closes it: water shoots from under the gate into the buckets and the wheel gathers way under their weight; shut, it runs on until the buckets are empty, then coasts to rest. The up and down arrows set the flow, and the readout gives the wheel's speed, the stone's and the output. It is also a progress indicator: the sacks of a batch fill with the progress, and on completion the sluice shuts and the wheel comes to rest. With reduced motion it holds still and jumps between states.
