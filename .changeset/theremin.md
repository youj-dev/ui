New miniature: the theremin, `MiniatureTheremin` from `@youj/miniatures/theremin`. A walnut theremin on tapered legs with a chrome pitch rod and a volume loop, played with your pointer as the hand: its distance from the rod sets the pitch and its height above the loop the volume. Soft rings gather around the rod and rise from the loop as it plays, and a small scope draws the wave. The arrow keys nudge the pitch and the volume, Space holds a note, V changes the vibrato and M plays a short original air by itself. Its optional sound is a sine with vibrato. With reduced motion the rings and the wave hold still, and the hand jumps from note to note.
