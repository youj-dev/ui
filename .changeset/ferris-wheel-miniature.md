New miniature: the Ferris wheel (`MiniatureFerrisWheel` from `@youj/miniatures/ferris-wheel`), for leisure, events and playful progress. A classic wheel on lattice A-frames, its colorful cars hanging level as it turns, with lights along the rim and spokes, a boarding deck and a ticket booth. Space starts or stops it and the arrow keys set its speed. Click a car to send it round to the boarding platform, where riders step on or off, and click it there to send it to the top; B brings the next car down. L cycles the lights through twinkle, chase, starburst and rainbow. It's also a progress indicator: `progress` turns the wheel that far round, its lights filling in behind car 1, and `done` brings car 1 back to the platform and lights everything. With reduced motion it steps a car at a time and jumps between states, with no flicker. With `sound` on, it plays a little waltz of its own while it runs.
