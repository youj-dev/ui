New miniature: the elevator (`MiniatureElevator`, `@youj/miniatures/elevator`), for steps, queues and multi-step flows. A traction elevator in a cutaway building section: the cab, its walls lit, rides steel ropes over the sheave on the roof against a counterweight, and each landing has brass doors, a dial whose needle counts the floors as the cab travels, a hall lantern and call buttons. The number keys send the cab to a floor and the arrow keys press call buttons, which stay lit until it comes; it collects calls on the way, speeds up and slows down smoothly, and the doors slide open on arrival with a chime. O holds the doors and C closes them. As a progress indicator the floors are the steps, and `done` takes the cab to the top.
