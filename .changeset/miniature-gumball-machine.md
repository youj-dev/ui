New miniature: the gumball machine (`MiniatureGumballMachine` from `@youj/miniatures/gumball-machine`), for rewards, small delights, and tokens or credits. A glass globe of bright gumballs sits on a red cast body with a coin slot and a chrome crank. Turn the crank with Space or Enter, a click or a drag, and a gumball drops out, rolls down a wire spiral round the post, swings the little flap door open and lands in the cup, its color in the readout. Each turn takes a coin (C drops one in). The globe empties as you go, D takes the gumballs from the cup and R lifts the cap to refill it. Under reduced motion every change is a jump.
