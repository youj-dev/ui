New miniature: the snow globe, `MiniatureSnowGlobe` from `@youj/miniatures/snow-globe`. A glass globe on a turned walnut base with a small brass plate and a winding key, holding a red log cabin with lit windows, pines, a lamp post and a skater on a frozen pond. Drag the globe or press Space to shake it: the snow whirls with the water and slowly settles, and the readout follows it. The arrow keys swirl it one way or the other, and W winds the music box, which plays an original waltz while the skater glides, slowing as the spring runs down. At night the windows and the lamp glow. With reduced motion the globe never moves and the snow settles in a few still steps.
