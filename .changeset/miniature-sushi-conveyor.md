New miniature: the sushi conveyor (`MiniatureSushiConveyor`, `@youj/miniatures/sushi-conveyor`), for queues, feeds, carousels, ordering and delivery screens. A conveyor-belt sushi counter: a crescent-slat belt carries colored plates of nigiri, maki and gunkan round a stainless island and past a wooden counter with an order screen, while the kitchen slides fresh plates out from under the noren of its window. Click a plate, or press Space for the one in front of you, to take it: your stack of empty plates grows, and the screen tallies them and the bill by plate color. The arrow keys slow the belt down or speed it up, O orders a fresh plate and C asks for the check. As a progress indicator your stack fills with the progress, and on completion the check comes. With reduced motion the belt holds still and every change jumps to its end.
