New miniature: the modular synth, `MiniatureModularSynth` from `@youj/miniatures/modular-synth`, for music, flows and screens about connecting things. A desktop Eurorack case between walnut cheeks, with a step sequencer, an oscillator, a filter, an envelope and an output module, and colored patch cables sagging between the jacks. The cables are the patch: each note's pulse runs from the sequencer down the cables to the modules they feed, lighting each one on the way to the output. Space runs or stops the sequencer, the number keys switch steps, the arrows pick and turn a knob, P steps through preset patches and Backspace pulls every cable. Drag from one jack to another to patch a cable, or press Enter on one jack and then on another. With reduced motion the modules on the signal path light steadily instead of pulsing, and nothing swings.
