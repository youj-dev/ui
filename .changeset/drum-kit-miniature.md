New miniature: the acoustic drum kit, `MiniatureDrumKit` from `@youj/miniatures/drum-kit`. A lacquered kit on a round rug with a kick, a snare, rack toms, a floor tom, a hi-hat, crash and ride cymbals and a stool. Press a key or click a drum to hit it: heads dip and ring, the kick pedal thumps, cymbals wobble on their felts and the hi-hat opens and closes. Space plays an original groove, the arrows set its tempo, and the readout shows the tempo and the last hit. Sound is opt-in, with synthesized drums. With reduced motion nothing travels: the hi-hat jumps open and shut, and heads and cymbals hold still.
