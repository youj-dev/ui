New miniature: the wind turbine (`MiniatureWindTurbine` from `@youj/miniatures/wind-turbine`), for energy dashboards and sustainability screens, a companion to the home battery. A tall three-blade turbine on a grassy rise beside a small substation; its readout climbs with the power it makes. Space sends a gust that spins the rotor up; ← and → turn the wind and the nacelle yaws to face it, its vane leading; ↑ and ↓ set the wind speed, with the blades pitching to hold the rated power and feathering above cut-out; B brakes, feathering the blades to stop the rotor, which restarts after a hold. Its aviation light blinks at night. It's also a progress indicator: `progress` sets the power as a share of the rated power and `done` runs it up to rated. With reduced motion the rotor holds still and every change jumps to its end.
