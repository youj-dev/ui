New miniature: the hot air balloon (`MiniatureHotAirBalloon` from `@youj/miniatures/hot-air-balloon`), for upgrades, travel, adventure and onboarding. Hold Space, or press and hold the balloon, to fire the burner: the flame roars, the envelope glows from inside and the balloon rises toward the top of its tether. Let go and it cools and slowly sinks, the basket swaying, until it lands on its patch. ← and → turn the wind and it drifts with it, and ↓ opens the vent. Left alone, the pilot holds a hover. It's also a progress indicator: `progress` sets the altitude and `done` takes it to the top of the tether. With reduced motion it jumps between altitudes, with no sway, flicker or drifting clouds.
