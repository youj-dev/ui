New miniature: the Mars rover, `MiniatureMarsRover` from `@youj/miniatures/mars-rover`. A six-wheeled rover on a patch of dusty red ground, with a solar deck, a mast with stereo camera eyes and a robotic arm. The up and down arrows drive it over rocks, its rocker-bogie letting each wheel climb on its own, and left and right turn it on the spot with its corner wheels steering in. The mast follows your pointer, Space unfolds the arm to core the nearest rock in a puff of dust, and P sweeps the mast for a panorama with a flash. The readout counts the sol, the distance, the samples and the frames. Left alone it drives, samples and looks around by itself; with reduced motion it holds still and every change lands at once.
