New miniature: the card catalog (`MiniatureCardCatalog`, `@youj/miniatures/card-catalog`), for search, archive, docs and knowledge-base screens. Type a letter and the oak drawer for it slides out, the index cards riffle forward and the first match rises with a highlight. Letters typed in quick succession narrow the search, and a query with no card leaves a dashed placeholder where it would be filed. Browse the cards with the arrow keys, take the found card out to read it, and push the drawer home with a soft thunk.
