New miniature: the claw machine (`MiniatureClawMachine`, `@youj/miniatures/claw-machine`), for rewards, prizes, loyalty and gamification screens. A glass case piled with plush toys under a lit marquee. Steer the claw with the arrow keys or the pointer over the glass, then press Space or the big button: the claw drops, closes, lifts and carries its catch home to the chute. Lined up well, it holds; off center, it may slip on the way; off target, it misses. A win tumbles down to the prize door while the marquee flashes. A coin adds a credit, P takes the prize, and left alone it plays itself.
