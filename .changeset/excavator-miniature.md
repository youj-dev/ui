New miniature: the excavator (`MiniatureExcavator` from `@youj/miniatures/excavator`), for maintenance pages, "under construction" screens and builds in progress. A compact tracked excavator with a dozer blade, a turning house with a glazed cab and a beacon, and a hydraulic boom, stick and bucket, between the trench it digs and a spoil pile. Space runs a dig cycle: the bucket bites and scoops, the cab swings round and dumps on the pile, and the trench deepens as the pile grows. The arrows work the boom and the swing, W and S the stick, C curls the bucket to scoop or dump, and H sounds the horn. Left alone it keeps digging and backfills the trench when the pile is full. It's also a progress indicator: `progress` drives the pile and `done` digs the last loads and parks it. With reduced motion it jumps between poses, with no flashing beacon, crumbs or exhaust.
