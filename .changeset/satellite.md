New miniature: the satellite (`MiniatureSatellite` from `@youj/miniatures/satellite`), for sync, uploads, telemetry and global or edge network screens. A communications satellite on a display stand, with a gold-foil bus, folding solar wings and a dish that beams down to a ground point on the floor while data packets run down the beam and back up. Space stows the wings against the bus and deploys them again, panel by panel, and on battery the link drops to a beacon. The arrow keys slew the dish (or click the floor to beam there), and T fires the thrusters. It's also a progress indicator: a sync fills a ring around the ground point, and on completion the link goes quiet. With reduced motion the wings and the dish jump to their positions and the packets hold still.
